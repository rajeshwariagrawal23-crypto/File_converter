FAQ

Good to know.

Can I convert PDF to Word?

Yes. Convertly extracts selectable text from a PDF and creates a DOCX. Original page styling, scanned text, and complex tables are not preserved.

Can I combine images into one PDF?

Yes. Select multiple JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP or SVG images and choose PDF. Each image becomes a separate page.

Do you upload my files?

No. Your files are processed locally in the browser. Conversion libraries are loaded from a CDN, so the first use needs an internet connection.
