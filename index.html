GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. All supported conversions happen locally in your browser.

Which conversions are supported?

PNG, JPG and WEBP image output, plus TXT, Markdown, HTML, JSON and CSV text output where compatible. Animated GIFs are converted using their first frame.

Can it convert PDF, Word, MP3 or MP4?

Not in this browser-only edition. Reliable PDF, Word, audio and video conversions need additional libraries or a backend. We don't claim to support formats we can't actually convert.
